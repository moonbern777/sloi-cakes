    Common questions

    How far ahead should I order?

    At least 3 days. For weekends and weddings, message me 2–3 weeks ahead, because I only take 6 cakes a week.

    How much cake does each person need?

    I plan on about 150 grams per guest. The calculator does this for you, and the smallest cake is 1.5 kg.

    How long does a cake keep?

    Up to 72 hours in the fridge. Before serving, let it sit for 30 minutes at room temperature.

    Can it be nut-free or lactose-free?

    Almost any flavor can be made without nuts. For other allergies, message me and we’ll adjust the recipe. This is a home kitchen, so I can’t rule out traces of allergens.
